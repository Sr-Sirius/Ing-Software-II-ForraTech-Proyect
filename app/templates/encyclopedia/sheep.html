Breed Overview

General information about sheep.

Sheep (Ovis aries) are ruminant mammals widely raised worldwide for their meat, milk, wool and hides. They are highly adaptable animals that thrive in diverse climates and environmental conditions.

  • Domestication: Over 10,000 years ago.
  • Distribution: Worldwide, in all continents.
  • Lifespan: 10–12 years on average.
  • Gestation: 145–155 days.
  • Social animals: Prefer to live in flocks.
  • Diet: Grazers, feed on grasses and legumes.

Key Characteristics

General characteristics of sheep.

  • Ruminant animals with a four-chambered stomach.
  • Excellent adaptation to various climates.
  • Good feed conversion efficiency.
  • Strong flocking and herd behavior.
  • High reproductive rate.
  • Wool or hair covering depending on breed.
