Forage Overview

General information about forages.

Forages are essential for livestock nutrition, offering energy, protein, vitamins and minerals. They can be grasses, legumes or other plants used for grazing, hay, silage or green chop.

  • Importance: Main source of nutrients for ruminants.
  • Adaptability: Grows in diverse climates and soils.
  • Forms: Grazing, hay, silage and green chop.
  • Sustainability: Improves soil health and fertility.
  • Management: Proper management increases productivity and quality.
