Breed Overview

General information about goats.

Goats (Capra hircus) are intelligent and adaptable ruminants known for their ability to thrive in diverse environments and provide high-quality products.

  • Domestication: Over 9,000 years ago.
  • Distribution: Worldwide, in all continents.
  • Lifespan: 10–15 years on average.
  • Gestation: 145–155 days.
  • Social animals: Prefer to live in herds.
  • Diet: Browsers, prefer shrubs, leaves and grasses.

Key Characteristics

General characteristics of goats.

  • Agile and excellent climbers.
  • Highly adaptable to harsh conditions.
  • Efficient browsers and good for brush control.
  • Strong maternal instinct.
  • Early sexual maturity.
  • Curious, intelligent and independent animals.
