What do these metrics mean for KNN?

Accuracy

Percentage of correct predictions made by the model.

Precision

How many of the predicted positives are actually correct.

Recall

How many of the actual positives were correctly found.

F1-Score

Balance between precision and recall.

ROC AUC

Measures the model’s ability to distinguish between classes.
