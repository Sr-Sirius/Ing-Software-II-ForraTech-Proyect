What do these results mean?

  • The terrain is highly suitable for warm-climate leguminous forages.
  • The posterior probability indicates high confidence in the recommendation.
  • Other varieties show lower posterior probability given the input conditions.
  • Gaussian distributions model each variety’s protein score behavior.
  • The ranking helps prioritize the best alternatives based on probability.
8.

Corrections and assumptions applied

  • Gaussian Naive Bayes assumes normal distribution of features per class.
  • Features are assumed conditionally independent given the variety.
  • Laplace smoothing is applied to handle zero probabilities.
  • Input values are normalized to ensure numerical stability.
  • DANE dataset is cleaned and balanced to reduce bias and improve inference.
