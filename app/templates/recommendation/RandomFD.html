What do these metrics mean for Random Forest?

Accuracy

Overall percentage of correct predictions for High or Low aptitude.

Precision

How many predicted High aptitude cases are truly High.

Recall

How many true High aptitude cases were correctly identified.

F1-Score

Balance between precision and recall.

ROC AUC

Measures the model’s ability to distinguish between classes.
