Detailed analysis by model

⌁ Logistic Regression

  • High interpretability: each variable coefficient shows its direct impact.
  • Sigmoid curve allows clear visualization of the decision threshold.
  • Limitation: assumes linear relationship between score and probability.

♙ Naive Bayes (Gaussian)

  • Assumes conditional independence between variables.
  • Fast training and low computational cost.
  • More conservative probabilities due to Gaussian distribution.

♧ Random Forest (200 trees)

  • Great capacity to model non-linear relationships and interactions.
  • Most robust model: high accuracy and generalization.
  • Lower interpretability compared to Logistic Regression.

How to interpret these metrics?

◎

Accuracy

Responds to “what percentage of times is the model correct?”. It is intuitive, but can be misleading if classes are unbalanced.

♧

Precision

Answers “when the model predicts optimal, how many really are?”. High precision avoids false positive recommendations.

♙

Recall

Answers “of all optimal terrains, how many does the model detect?”. High recall avoids missing good terrains.

⌁

ROC-AUC

Measures the model’s ability to rank classes correctly across all thresholds.

▣

Cross-validation

Evaluates model stability on different data subsets. Lower variance indicates a more reliable model.
