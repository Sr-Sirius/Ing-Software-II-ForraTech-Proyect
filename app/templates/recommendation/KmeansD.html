What do these metrics mean for K-Means?

Accuracy

Percentage of data points correctly assigned to their natural cluster.

Precision

How pure the clusters are, meaning how similar the points are within each cluster.

Recall

How complete the clusters are, meaning how well the points that belong together are grouped.

F1-Score

Harmonic mean between precision and recall, providing a balanced evaluation.

ROC AUC

Measures the clustering model’s ability to discriminate between different groups.
